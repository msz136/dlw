(2+1)-Dimensional Spaces∗

  LI De-Sheng1,2,† and ZHANG Hong-Qing1

  (Received October 16, 2002; Revised January 8, 2003)
   Abstract        In this paper, by using a further extended tanh method and symbolic computation system, some new
   soliton-like and period form solutions of the dispersive long-wave equation in (2+1)-dimensional spaces are obtained.

   Key words: further extended tanh method, symbolic computation system, soliton-like solutions, period form
              solutions, solitary waves
