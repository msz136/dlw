               Symmetry Reducibility of （2 ＋ 1） Dimensional
      Dispersive Long Wave System and the Group-invariant Solution

                                                      DU Hai-qing
                        （ Department of Mathematics，Huaiyin Teachers’ College，Huaian 223300，China）


Abstract： In this paper，we study on the （2 ＋ 1）dimensional dispersive long-wave system． First，we determine
the symmetry group of the system． It turns out that the symmetry group contains three arbitrary smooth functions．
Then，we reduce the system to the heat equation and the second Painlevé equation by using some subgroups．
Key words： （2 ＋ 1）dimensional dispersive long wave system；symmetry group；group invariant solution
