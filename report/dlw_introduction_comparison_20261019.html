1. 研究背景与研究对象

新版第1段为新增背景，第2段改写研究对象的介绍。

修改前

修改前 · 第 1 段

English 原文

The (2+1)-dimensional dispersive long wave (DLW) system was introduced by Boiti, Leon and Pempinelli [1] to model the hydrodynamics of a wide channel or of the open sea of finite depth. Its two dependent variables carry a direct physical meaning: u is the horizontal velocity and v the elevation of the free surface above the undisturbed level, while x and y are the two horizontal coordinates of the propagation plane and t is time. Because the two space directions enter the equations differently, the nonlinear waves are oblique bands whose orientation and propagation speed can be chosen independently, and their interaction is genuinely two dimensional rather than a superposition of one-dimensional profiles. The system is a two-dimensional extension of the (1+1)-dimensional dispersive long wave equation, and the reduction y=x collapses the pair to a (1+1)-dimensional long-wave system.

中文对应

（2+1）维色散长波（DLW）系统由 Boiti、Leon 与 Pempinelli 提出 [1]，用于描述宽水道或有限深外海的流体动力学。它的两个因变量具有直接的物理含义：u 为水平速度，v 为自由水面相对静水面的抬升；x、y 为传播平面内的两个水平坐标，t 为时间。由于两个空间方向以不同方式进入方程，非线性波表现为斜向波带，其取向与传播速度可以独立选取，波与波的相互作用是真正的二维过程，而非一维波形的叠加。该系统是（1+1）维色散长波方程的二维推广，作约化 y=x 后两个方程退化为一个（1+1）维长波系统。

修改后

修改后 · 第 1 段

English 原文

Nonlinear long-wave equations provide a setting in which dispersion, nonlinear transport and coherent wave propagation can be studied together. In two spatial dimensions, transverse variation introduces an additional question: how does the spatial geometry of a wave affect its propagation and interaction with other waves? Integrable models are particularly useful for this purpose because their exact solutions and auxiliary linear problems allow these effects to be examined analytically. They also provide demanding numerical benchmarks: a computed wave must reproduce its position and shape in a plane, together with the coupled fields that determine its evolution. The (2+1)-dimensional dispersive long wave (DLW) system considered here combines this multidimensional wave structure with an explicit bilinear representation.

中文对应

非线性长波方程为研究色散、非线性输运与相干波传播提供了共同框架。在两个空间维度中，横向变化引出了进一步的问题：波的空间几何如何影响其传播及与其他波的相互作用？可积模型的精确解与辅助线性问题使这些效应能够得到解析研究，也为数值计算提供了严格的参照：计算不仅需要再现波在平面内的位置与形状，还需要正确描述决定其演化的耦合场。本文考虑的（2+1）维色散长波（DLW）系统兼具这种多维波结构与显式的双线性表示。

修改后 · 第 2 段

English 原文

Boiti, Leon and Pempinelli introduced the system as a two-spatial-dimensional extension of the dispersive long wave equation and developed its inverse spectral transform [1]. In the long-wave interpretation used in subsequent studies, the system describes wave motion in wide channels or open seas of finite depth [3,4]. We work with the normalized two-field form specified in Section 2.1, following [5]. This choice fixes the background and scaling conventions for the physical fields u and v. The two horizontal coordinates enter the equations asymmetrically: mixed derivatives involving y are coupled to transport and higher derivatives in x. Consequently, retaining the transverse dependence is important both for the analytical construction and for its numerical realization. Line-soliton solutions give a particularly accessible example, since their crests form oblique bands in the spatial plane and their intersections test the treatment of transverse coupling.

中文对应

Boiti、Leon 与 Pempinelli 将该系统作为色散长波方程的二维空间推广提出，并建立了其逆谱变换 [1]。后续研究中的长波解释将其用于描述宽水道或有限深外海中的波动 [3,4]。本文采用文献 [5] 的归一化双场形式，具体方程见第 2.1 节；这一选择固定了物理场 u、v 的背景与尺度约定。两个水平坐标以不对称方式进入方程：含 y 的混合导数与 x 方向的输运及高阶导数相耦合。因此，解析构造及数值实现均需要保留横向依赖。线孤子提供了直观例子，其波峰在空间平面内形成斜向波带，波带交汇则检验了算法处理横向耦合的能力。

2. 已有解析研究及 sinh-Gordon 联系

原来的一段展开为多个段落；新版第5段新增近年对称性研究。

修改前

修改前 · 第 2 段

English 原文

Since its introduction the system has been studied mainly through its exact solutions. The original paper developed a spectral transform [1]; nonlinear transformations of the dependent variables [2] and bilinear Bäcklund transformations [3] followed. Determinant representations were obtained within the Sato and Hirota frameworks [4], and a reduction of the KP hierarchy then produced Gram determinant solutions describing solitons, line breathers and rational waves on constant and periodic backgrounds [5]. These results all express the nonlinear physical fields through a common τ-function structure. The bilinear form is connected with the modified KP hierarchy, and it is closely related to that of the (2+1)-dimensional sinh-Gordon equation: interchanging the roles of y and t, together with a suitable choice of the parameters, carries one bilinear system into the other [5]. This is more than a curiosity, because the bilinear sinh-Gordon system already has integrable semi-discrete and fully discrete versions, together with their Bäcklund transformations and Lax pairs [6]. The DLW system is therefore a natural next target: the technique is known, but the staggered structure that two space directions require is not.

中文对应

自提出以来，该系统的研究主要围绕其精确解展开。原始文献建立了谱变换 [1]；随后给出了因变量的非线性变换 [2] 与双线性 Bäcklund 变换 [3]。在 Sato 理论与 Hirota 方法框架下得到了行列式表示 [4]；进一步通过 KP 层级约化，得到了常值背景与周期背景上描述孤子、线呼吸子与有理波的 Gram 行列式解 [5]。这些结果都将非线性物理场表达为共同的 τ 函数结构。其双线性形式与修正 KP 层级相联系，并与（2+1）维 sinh-Gordon 方程的双线性形式密切相关：交换 y 与 t 的角色并选取适当参数，可由其中一个双线性系统得到另一个 [5]。这一点并非巧合，因为 sinh-Gordon 的双线性系统已有可积的半离散与全离散版本，以及相应的 Bäcklund 变换与 Lax 对 [6]。因此 DLW 系统是一个自然的下一步：方法已知，但两个空间方向所要求的交错结构并不现成。

修改后 · 第 3 段

English 原文

The analytical study of this system has developed through several complementary approaches. The original spectral formulation supplied a Bäcklund transformation, soliton solutions and a superposition formula [1]. Wang, Zhou and Li subsequently introduced a nonlinear transformation for constructing solutions [2], while Sun et al. obtained a bilinear representation and a bilinear auto-Bäcklund transformation using symbolic computation and generalized Bell polynomials [3]. These developments relate the nonlinear equations to auxiliary functions and transformation rules, making it possible to generate solutions without solving the original coupled equations directly. For discretization, this is a useful starting point: the transformation structure supplies algebraic relations that can be retained when a continuous spatial variable is replaced by a lattice.

中文对应

该系统的解析研究沿若干互补方向发展。最初的谱方法给出了 Bäcklund 变换、孤子解与叠加公式 [1]。Wang、Zhou 与 Li 随后引入了构造解的非线性变换 [2]；Sun 等结合符号计算与广义 Bell 多项式，获得了双线性表示及双线性自 Bäcklund 变换 [3]。这些工作通过辅助函数与变换规则联系非线性方程，使解的生成不必依赖直接求解原耦合系统。对于离散化而言，这提供了有用的起点：将连续空间变量替换为格点时，可以尝试保留变换结构所给出的代数关系。

修改后 · 第 4 段

English 原文

Determinant formulas organize this solution structure for arbitrary finite numbers of interacting components. Hu, Xu and Yu constructed both Grammian and Wronskian solutions using Sato theory and Hirota's bilinear method, and related their construction to the Boussinesq–Burgers system through dimensional reduction [4]. Sheng and Yu developed Gram determinant solutions through a KP-hierarchy reduction, including solitons, line breathers and rational solutions on constant and periodic backgrounds [5]. Their continuous determinant family is the starting point of the present work. The representation is also useful computationally: the same parameters specify the wave profiles and provide explicit reference fields against which spatial and temporal errors can be measured.

中文对应

行列式公式将任意有限个相互作用分量的解纳入统一表示。Hu、Xu 与 Yu 利用 Sato 理论及 Hirota 双线性方法，构造了 Grammian 与 Wronskian 解，并通过降维约化联系到 Boussinesq–Burgers 系统 [4]。Sheng 与 Yu 通过 KP 层级约化发展了 Gram 行列式解，包括常值与周期背景上的孤子、线呼吸子及有理解 [5]。本文以其连续行列式解族为出发点。这一表示也适用于数值研究：同一组参数既确定波形，又给出可用于测量空间及时间误差的显式参照场。

English 原文

Research on the associated Boiti–Leon–Pempinelli system has also continued beyond individual solution formulas. Maltseva and Popovych computed its point-symmetry pseudogroup, classified Lie reductions and enlarged exact-solution families using differential constraints and Laplace and Darboux transformations [11]. Their discussion of equivalent formulations makes the dependence on variable transformations explicit. This is relevant when comparing results across the DLW literature, where different normalizations and auxiliary variables occur. In the present setting, the corresponding issue arises after discretization: a lattice equation for auxiliary quantities must be accompanied by a definite reconstruction of the physical fields before its continuum limit or numerical accuracy can be assessed.

相关 Boiti–Leon–Pempinelli 系统的研究也持续超出单个解公式的构造。Maltseva 与 Popovych 计算了其点对称伪群，分类了 Lie 约化，并利用微分约束及 Laplace、Darboux 变换扩展了精确解族 [11]。他们对等价表示的讨论明确说明了变量变换的作用。这对比较 DLW 文献中的不同归一化及辅助变量尤其重要。本文在离散层面面临相应问题：辅助量的格点方程必须配合明确的物理场重构，才能讨论连续极限或数值精度。

English 原文

For DLW, a further guide is the relation between its bilinear equations and those of the (2+1)-dimensional sinh-Gordon system discussed in [5]. Hu and Yu constructed integrable semi-discrete and fully discrete versions of the latter, together with determinant solutions and Bäcklund–Lax structures [6]. These connections suggest using shifts of both the lattice argument and the bilinear parameter. The task for the DLW evolution considered here is to select these shifts consistently with a lattice in y, while keeping x and the physical time t continuous. 对于 DLW，文献 [5] 所讨论的其双线性方程与（2+1）维 sinh-Gordon 系统的联系提供了进一步线索。Hu 与 Yu 已构造后者的可积半离散及全离散版本，并给出行列式解及 Bäcklund–Lax 结构 [6]。这些联系启发我们同时平移格点自变量与双线性参数。对于本文考虑的 DLW 演化，需要使这些平移与 y 方向的格点一致，同时保持 x 及物理时间 t 连续。随后还须通过重构保留两个物理场之间的关系，并在格距趋于零时恢复所选的连续归一化形式。

3. 可积离散化、误差参照与动网格

将原段的多个论点拆开论述，补充方法背景、误差参照及二维网格条件。

修改前

修改前 · 第 3 段

English 原文

The integrable discretization of soliton equations has a long record, and Hirota’s bilinear method provides one of its most systematic routes [6–8]. The compatibility between a bilinear equation and a Bäcklund transformation yields semi-discrete analogues of several soliton equations, including extended KdV and KP equations [7], and in (2+1) dimensions bilinear constructions have produced a semi-discrete system related to the KP and Zakharov equations, admitting N-soliton solutions and soliton resonance [8]. Two features make such discretizations more than formal exercises. First, the exact solutions of the discrete system are the correct benchmarks for a numerical scheme: what a scheme approximates is the discrete solution, not the continuous one, so without them the accuracy of a scheme can only be measured against a model that the scheme was not built for. Second, a bilinear system usually comes with conservation laws, and a conserved density can be used directly as a mesh density. This is the idea behind the self-adaptive moving mesh method (SAMM) developed for semi-discrete integrable systems in (1+1) dimensions [9], where the profiles are straight lines and a single sliding coordinate suffices. In two space dimensions the profiles are oblique bands, and because all y layers must share the same x nodes, the density has to be averaged across the transverse direction; the construction then has to be re-derived rather than copied.

中文对应

孤子方程的可积离散化研究由来已久，Hirota 双线性方法是其中最系统的途径之一 [6–8]。双线性方程与 Bäcklund 变换的相容性可导出多个孤子方程的半离散对应，包括扩展 KdV 与 KP 方程 [7]；在（2+1）维情形，双线性构造已给出与 KP、Zakharov 方程相关的半离散系统，并具有 N 孤子解与孤子共振 [8]。这类离散化之所以并非形式练习，原因有两点。其一，离散系统的精确解才是数值格式的正确基准：格式所逼近的是离散解而非连续解，缺少离散精确解，就只能用格式本来并非针对的模型去衡量其精度。其二，双线性系统通常附带守恒律，而守恒密度可以直接用作网格密度——这正是（1+1）维半离散可积系统的自适应动网格方法（SAMM）的出发点 [9]。在一维情形解曲线是直线，单一滑动坐标即可；在二维情形解为斜向波带，且所有 y 层必须共用同一套 x 节点，密度须沿横向平均，构造需要重新推导而不能照搬。

修改后 · 第 6 段

English 原文

Integrable discretization addresses how much of this analytical structure can survive on a lattice. A Taylor-consistent difference approximation specifies a continuum limit, whereas a compatible auxiliary linear system imposes additional relations among the discrete equations. Hirota's direct method provides a systematic language for constructing such relations in terms of bilinear equations and determinant identities [16]. Zhang et al. used compatibility with bilinear Bäcklund transformations to derive semi-discrete analogues of several soliton equations, including extended KdV and KP systems, together with their Lax pairs and continuum limits [7]. In two spatial dimensions, Yu and Xu constructed a differential-difference system related to the KP and Zakharov equations and studied its soliton dynamics [8]. These examples motivate a construction in which the lattice equations, exact solutions and linear compatibility conditions are developed together.

中文对应

可积离散化关心上述解析结构能够在多大程度上保留到格点上。Taylor 相容的差分近似规定了连续极限，而相容的辅助线性系统进一步约束了离散方程之间的关系。Hirota 直接法以双线性方程及行列式恒等式为这类构造提供了系统语言 [16]。Zhang 等利用双线性 Bäcklund 变换的相容性，导出了包括扩展 KdV 与 KP 系统在内的若干孤子方程的半离散对应物，并给出 Lax 对及连续极限 [7]。在两个空间维度中，Yu 与 Xu 构造了与 KP、Zakharov 方程相关的微分差分系统，并研究了其孤子动力学 [8]。这些例子启发我们将格点方程、精确解与线性相容条件结合起来构造。

English 原文

The relation between exact solutions at the two levels is essential to this comparison. A numerical solution of a semi-discrete system contains errors from the subsequent discretization in x and time; its difference from a continuous DLW solution also reflects the change introduced by the y lattice. Exact semi-discrete solutions allow the former errors to be examined, while exact continuous solutions provide a reference for the overall approximation to the PDE. Neither reference replaces the other. A continuum expansion of the reconstructed determinant solutions identifies the leading difference between these reference families and helps interpret an observed second-order trend. It also makes the analytical scope precise: convergence of a regular finite-dimensional family of exact solutions does not by itself establish stability or convergence of a numerical method for arbitrary initial data.

中文对应

两个层次的精确解之间的关系对这一比较至关重要。半离散系统的数值解包含后续 x 方向及时间离散带来的误差；它与连续 DLW 解之间的差异还包含引入 y 格点造成的变化。半离散精确解可以帮助考察前一类误差，连续精确解则为逼近原 PDE 的总误差提供参照，两者各有作用。重构行列式解的连续极限展开给出了两个参照解族之差的首项，有助于解释观测到的二阶趋势，也明确了分析的范围：正则有限维精确解族的收敛本身并不构成任意初值下数值算法稳定性或收敛性的证明。

修改后 · 第 10 段

English 原文

There is a second numerical motivation for retaining analytical structure. A uniform grid may allocate many points to weakly varying regions while resolving a narrow wave band inadequately. Moving mesh methods redistribute a fixed number of nodes through a solution-dependent density; their general framework includes the choice of a monitor, an equidistribution principle and an evolution law for the mesh [15]. Within integrable systems, conservation laws give a particularly direct connection between the evolving solution and the node distribution. Feng, Maruno and Ohta developed a self-adaptive method for the Camassa–Holm equation [13] and related moving-mesh schemes for short pulse equations to discrete reciprocal transformations and Lax pairs [14]. Feng, Sheng and Yu subsequently constructed integrable semi-discretizations and moving-mesh methods for a generalized sine-Gordon equation [9]. These studies motivate using a conservation density as part of the DLW numerical construction.

中文对应

保留解析结构还有另一项数值动机。均匀网格可能在变化较弱的区域分配大量节点，却未能充分分辨狭窄波带。动网格方法通过依赖解的密度重新分配固定数量的节点，其一般框架包括监测函数选择、等分布原则及网格演化律 [15]。在可积系统中，守恒律为演化解与节点分布建立了直接联系。Feng、Maruno 与 Ohta 提出了 Camassa–Holm 方程的自适应方法 [13]，并将短脉冲方程的动网格格式与离散倒易变换及 Lax 对联系起来 [14]。Feng、Sheng 与 Yu 随后为广义 sine-Gordon 方程构造了可积半离散化与动网格方法 [9]。这些工作启发我们将守恒密度纳入 DLW 的数值构造。

English 原文

The present two-dimensional setting requires a specific adaptation of that idea. We keep a uniform lattice in y and use a common set of moving x nodes for all transverse layers. A density chosen separately on each layer would generally prescribe different node positions, so we average the semi-discrete conservation density and its flux across the layers. The resulting one-dimensional conservation law determines the shared node velocity through conservation of cumulative density. Positivity of the averaged density and preservation of node ordering are required for this coordinate description. This gives a mesh suited to the tensor-product representation of the fields, although it does not adapt the y resolution or align an arbitrary mesh with each individual wave crest. Its numerical benefit must therefore be assessed for the wave geometries actually tested.

中文对应

本文的二维设置需要对这一思路作具体调整。我们保留均匀的 y 格点，并使所有横向层共用一组移动的 x 节点。各层独立选择的密度一般会给出不同的节点位置，因此我们对半离散守恒密度及其通量作横向平均。所得一维守恒律通过累积密度守恒确定共用节点的速度。这一坐标描述要求平均密度为正，并保持节点有序。所得网格适合场的张量积表示，但并不调整 y 方向的分辨率，也不使任意网格逐一贴合每条波峰；其数值收益因而需要结合实际测试的波形几何来评价。

4. 已有工作与本文问题的定位

修正原稿对离散DLW研究空白的笼统判断。

修改前

修改前 · 第 4 段

English 原文

What has been missing for the (2+1)-dimensional DLW system is the discretization itself. To our knowledge there is no semi-discrete bilinear form for this system, hence no determinant solution of the discrete system, no Lax representation of the discrete equations, and no estimate of how the discrete solutions approach the continuous ones as the lattice spacing vanishes. Each of these is needed before a numerical method built on the discrete system can be trusted: without the determinant solution there is no exact reference at the discrete level, and without a continuum-limit estimate the relation between the discrete and the continuous problems is only presumed.

中文对应

对（2+1）维 DLW 系统而言，缺的正是离散化本身。据我们所知，该系统的半离散双线性形式尚未给出，因而也没有离散系统的行列式解、离散方程的 Lax 表示，以及格距趋于零时离散解如何逼近连续解的估计。在这些结果之前，建立在离散系统之上的数值方法无法被信任：没有行列式解，离散层面就没有精确参照；没有连续极限估计，离散问题与连续问题的关系只是假定。

English 原文

Discrete DLW-related systems already occur in the literature. In particular, Tian et al. studied Lie symmetries and nonlocally related systems for continuous and discrete dispersive long-wave equations [12]. Their discrete system is expressed through a lattice index and two continuous variables, so a general claim that DLW has no discrete counterpart would be inappropriate. The problem addressed here is more specific: to construct a staggered semi-discrete realization of the bilinear system in Section 2.1, establish its determinant and Darboux–Lax representations, and quantify the relation between its reconstructed fields and the continuous DLW fields. English 原文

Our aim is to connect the integrable construction to this numerical use within one consistent formulation. The semi-discrete equations admit two nonlinear representations: the potential-elimination formulation (PE) and the potential formulation (PF). Their common origin provides matched exact solutions, while their different auxiliary variables and reconstruction steps lead to different numerical implementations. We compare both with direct finite differences applied to the continuous DLW equations. The spatial formulation, time integrator and mesh motion are varied in separate comparisons, since a Lax representation for the semi-discrete model alone does not determine the accuracy of its fully numerical implementation. The numerical study uses four one- and two-soliton tests and measures errors in both physical fields.
