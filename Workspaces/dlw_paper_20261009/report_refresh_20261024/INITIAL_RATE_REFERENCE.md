# Exact initial-rate reference for the two non-soliton data

## Scope

These calculations evaluate the continuous DLW operator on the specified initial
fields. They do not construct a solution at positive time and do not assume that
the positive-time initial-boundary value problem has already been proved
well posed. If a sufficiently smooth solution with the prescribed lower trace
exists, its initial time derivatives must equal the formulas below. The formulas
are independently meaningful as the reference for initial spatial defects.

Parameters are a=2 and lambda=-2. The periodic x interval is [-96,96); the y
interval is [-2,2]. The prescribed lower trace is u(x,-2,t)=0, so its time
derivative is zero. Write

\[
e(y)=\sin\frac{\pi(y+2)}4,\qquad
E(y)=\int_{-2}^{y}e(s)\,ds
=\frac4\pi\left(1-\cos\frac{\pi(y+2)}4\right),
\quad u_0=e(y)p(x),\quad v_0=e(y)q(x).
\]

The periodic Gaussian uses the user-approved exact profile

\[
G(x)=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}\exp[-(x+192m)^2/36],\quad
p=.05G,\quad q=.03G.
\]

The sinusoidal data are p=.03 cos(pi x/12), q=.02 sin(pi x/12).

## Derivation

The continuous system is

\[
u_{yt}=-v_{xx}-\partial_x[(u+4)u_y],\qquad
v_t=-u_{xxy}-\partial_x[(u+4)v-4u].
\]

Since (u+4)u_y=partial_y(u^2/2+4u), integration from y=-2, together
with the lower trace, gives

\[
u_t=-\int_{-2}^yv_{xx}(x,s,t)\,ds-(u+4)u_x.
\]

At initialization the exact rates are therefore

\[
R_u(x,y)=-E(y)q''(x)-(e(y)p(x)+4)e(y)p'(x),
\]
\[
R_v(x,y)=-e'(y)p''(x)-e(y)^2p'(x)q(x)
 -(e(y)p(x)+4)e(y)q'(x)+4e(y)p'(x).
\]

No spatial numerical differentiation or numerical quadrature is used to evaluate
these references. The Gaussian derivatives are obtained by differentiating its
absolutely and uniformly convergent periodic sum.

## Numerical methods and measurement

The script imports the current Fourier x operators and Endpoint-SD/FD right-hand
sides from spatial_audit_20261023/run.py. It overrides the old a=1/3 setting with
a=2 and replaces the original unperiodized Gaussian profile with G above.
The exact lower u_t constant is zero at the actual first u node y=-2.

Centered-SD imports the full-potential right-hand side and the physical v_t
chain rule from separated/reconstruction_probe/rerun_20261024.py. The physical
initial u and v are sampled from the same continuous fields as the other methods.
The lower auxiliary value is w_0=v_0-u_{0,y}. The inverse uses the same map,
initial iterate, damping 1/2 and residual threshold 2e-12 as the existing code.
Only its iteration cap is increased from 200 to 1000; the longest Gaussian solve
takes 223 iterations. Every w node is included in its RHS, including the lower
node, exactly as in the current soliton code.

The table reports max sampled errors in R_u and R_v at h=dx=.1 over the whole
periodic x grid and the respective field sites satisfying |y|<=1.8. The u sites
are y=-2+jh. Endpoint-SD v sites are y_j-h/2 and Centered-SD v sites are the
internal edge midpoints; their measured edge sites coincide. FD v sites are the
u nodes. These are sampled maximum norms, not certified maxima between nodes.

The Centered-SD reconstructed-v residual is below 1e-12 in both cases. Its first
equation agrees with the endpoint-form physical u equation to below 9e-14.
An independent finite-difference check of the nonlinear field-map derivative
agrees with the implemented v_t chain rule to below 8e-11.

## Certified reference evaluation

`initial_non_soliton_reference_certificate.json` is generated by evaluating
every reference grid point with Arb at 80 decimal digits. The abscissae are the
exact binary64 coordinates used in the numerical samples, not rounded decimal
approximations to those coordinates. Each case has 1920 x sites and 99 distinct
y coordinates (including harmless floating-point differences between equivalent
node constructions), giving 380160 validated rate values.

For the Gaussian, the double-precision evaluator retains m=-1,0,1. All omitted
terms satisfy |x+192m|>=288 for |x|<=96. Set

\[
Q=\frac{25}{9}e^{-4096},\quad
T_0=\frac{2e^{-2304}}{1-Q},\quad
T_1=\frac{32e^{-2304}}{1-Q},\quad
T_2=\frac{2(256+1/18)e^{-2304}}{1-Q}.
\]

These bound the omitted tails of G,G',G'', respectively. The polynomial factor
in the derivative tails has consecutive ratio at most (5/3)^2, while the
Gaussian factor has ratio at most exp(-4096). Elementary bounds |e|<=1,
|e'|<1, |E|<3 and |G|<1.1, |G'|<1, |G''|<1 imply that each rate-tail
contribution is bounded by 100(T_0+T_1+T_2)<1e-990. This contribution is added to
the Arb evaluation error before the final comparison.

For every checked reference value in both cases,

\[
|\widehat R_u-R_u|<10^{-14},\qquad
|\widehat R_v-R_v|<10^{-14}.
\]

The largest outward error bounds found are approximately 1.75e-17/1.05e-17
(Gaussian u_t/v_t) and 1.42e-16/1.67e-16 (sinusoidal u_t/v_t).
The quoted 1e-14 bounds are conservative certified bounds. This certification
applies to the analytic reference evaluation only. The FFTs, nonlinear inverse
and final defect subtraction are ordinary binary64 calculations and have not
been enclosed by interval arithmetic. No claim of certified positive-time
continuous solution accuracy follows from these initial-rate certificates.
