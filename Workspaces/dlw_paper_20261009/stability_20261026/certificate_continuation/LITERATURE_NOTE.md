# Literature check, 2026-10-26

Public primary-author dissertation synopsis by T. I. Garagash (1995):
https://fizmathim.com/pryamaya-i-obratnaya-zadachi-dlya-2-1-mernogo-nelineynogo-uravneniya-blp

The synopsis describes scattering for real Schwartz potentials around the free operator containing a constant term. It expressly assumes unique solvability of its resolvent integral equations and the absence of additional singularities, rather than proving those hypotheses for all Schwartz data. Its evolution discussion imposes strong spectral decay near the transverse zero frequency and at large longitudinal frequency. The displayed formulas suffer OCR corruption; no exact formula is copied into our proof from that transcription.

This is useful evidence that an integrable representation does not automatically settle the Cauchy problem. It is not an applicable quantitative theorem for our periodic strip, constant-4 physical background, prescribed lower trace, or independent perturbations.

The 1996 paper's ResearchGate page labels a file as full text, but the readable displayed content only contains its reference list; the PDF link failed. We have not treated that as access to the full paper. Prior publisher abstracts likewise do not supply the missing theorem. No literature-based reference certificate has been obtained.
