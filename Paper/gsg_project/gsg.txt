Integrable semi-discretizations and self-adaptive
moving mesh method for a generalized sine-Gordon
equation

Bao-Feng Feng1 · Han-Han Sheng2 · Guo-Fu Yu2

Received: 5 August 2022 / Accepted: 20 January 2023 / Published online: 23 February 2023

Abstract
In the present paper, two integrable and one non-integrable semi-discrete analogues
of a generalized sine-Gordon (sG) equation are constructed. The keys of the con-
struction are the Bäcklund transformation of bilinear equations and appropriate
definition of the discrete hodograph transformation. We construct N-soliton solutions
for the semi-discrete analogues of the generalized sG equation in the determinant
form. In the continuous limit, we show that the semi-discrete generalized sG equa-
tions converge to the continuous generalized sG equation. Furthermore, we propose
four self-adaptive moving mesh methods for the generalized sG equation, two are
integrable and two are non-integrable. Integrable and non-integrable self-adaptive
moving mesh methods are proposed and used for simulations of regular, irregular and
loop soliton while comparing with the Crank-Nicolson (C-N) scheme. The numerical
solutions show that the proposed self-adaptive moving methods perform better than
the C-N scheme.

Keywords Generalized sG equation · Integrable discretization ·
Discrete hodograph transformation · Self-adaptive moving mesh method

1 Introduction

In this paper, we consider the integrable semi-discretizations of the following
generalized sine-Gordon (sG) equation


                              uxt = (1 + ν∂x2 ) sin u,                                   (1.1)


where u = u(x, t) is a scalar-valued function, ν is a real parameter, ∂x2 and the
subscripts t and x appended to u denote partial differentiation. The generalized sG
equation was derived for the first time in [1] using bi-Hamiltonian method. In the
case ν = −1, the integrability was shown with Lax pair and initial value problem of
the equation was solved for decaying initial data [2]. The Riemann-Hilbert formalism
was developed to obtain eigenfunctions of the Lax pair and construct traveling-wave
solutions in [2]. Bilinear transformation method was used to construct multi-soliton
solutions in the form of parametric representation and a variety of solutions which
include kinks, loop solitons and breathers were obtained in [3]. Equation (1.1) with
ν = 1 was solved by Hirota’s bilinear method [4]. It was shown that the equation
exhibits kink and breather solutions and does not admit multi-valued solutions like
loop solitons as obtained when ν = −1.
   It was shown that the generalized sG equation reduces to the short pulse (SP) equa-
tion in the short wave limit, and reduces to the sG equation in the long wave limit
[3]. Therefore, we can say that the generalized sG equation is an interesting soli-
ton equation lies between the SP equation and the sG equation. Due to the facts that
the discrete sG equation has been well-known [5, 6], and the integrable discretiza-
tions of the SP equation was recently proposed in [7], it would be an interesting
problem to look for the integrable discretization of the generalized sG equation.
Integrable discretizations of soliton equations have received considerable attention
recently [8–11]. Integrable semi- and fully discretizations of the SP equation [7],
semi-discretization of (2+1)-dimensional Zakharov equation [12] were constructed
via Hirota’s bilinear method. Based on the compatibility between an integrable
system and its Bäcklund transformation, a systematic procedure to derive discrete
analogues of integrable PDEs via Hirota’s bilinear method was proposed [13].
Most recently, an integrable discretization for a coupled SP equation [14, 15] was
constructed [16].
   In this paper, we attempt to construct integrable semi-discrete analogues of the
generalized sG equation (1.1) with ν = −1 by the same approach used in the
Camassa-Holm equation [17] and SP equation [7], and apply them for the numerical
simulations as integrable self-adaptive moving mesh (SAMM) method.
   The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we review the bilinear equations
and determinant solutions of the generalized sG equation with ν = −1. In Section 3,
we propose two integrable semi-discrete analogues, whose N-soliton solutions are
also constructed in terms of determinant form. We then apply the two versions of the
semi-discrete generalized sG equations as integrable SAMM method while compar-
ing with a non-integrable SAMM method. Section 4 is devoted to conclusions and
some discussions.
