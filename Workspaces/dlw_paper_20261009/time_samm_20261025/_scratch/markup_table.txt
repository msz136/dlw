<section id="section-2"><h2>2. Bilinear lattice construction and exact solutions</h2><p>In this section, we develop a bilinear lattice description of the DLW system that connects the continuous equations, the arrangement of the τ functions and the construction of exact solutions. In Section 2.1, we recall the continuous system and its bilinear representation, fixing the parameters and the transformation to the physical fields. We then derive an unreduced bilinear lattice from the Hirota–Miwa equation in Section 2.2, making the origin of the discrete relations explicit. Building on these relations, Section 2.3 introduces a symmetric staggered formulation through a shift of the bilinear parameter. In Section 2.4, we construct the corresponding Gram determinant solutions and specify the conditions under which they yield regular physical fields.
