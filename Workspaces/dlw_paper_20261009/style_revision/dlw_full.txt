In this paper, we investigate a (2+1)-dimensional dispersive long wave system with an arbitrary con-
stant a. Solitons, line breathers, rational solutions and algebraic solitons of the system are constructed
based on Hirota’s bilinear method and Kadomtsev–Petviashvili (KP) hierarchy reduction technique.
These solutions are expressed in terms of N × N determinant. When the size of the determinant
N is odd, solutions on periodic backgrounds are generated. Solutions on constant backgrounds are
derived when N is even. By using asymptotic analysis, we elucidate explicit expressions of asymptotic
algebraic solitons localized in the straight for the algebraic soliton solutions. As a byproduct, we find
lump solutions for an intermediate potential of the system. The effects of constant a are discussed and
it is shown that the constant can affect collision of solitons. Dynamics of the obtained solutions are
discussed with plots.

1. Introduction
In the 1960s, Zabusky and Kruskal used finite difference approach to study numerical solutions of KdV equation and introduced
the concept of solitons [1]. Solitons have the prototype elastic collision property, that is, the shapes and velocities keep the same after
collisions. Besides KdV equation, solitons exist in other (1+1)-dimensional continuous and discrete equations, including the modified
KdV, the Burgers equation, the sine–Gordon equation, the nonlinear Schrödinger equation and their higher dimensional extensions.
These equations have been widely used to model nonlinear phenomena in physics, optics, biology and chemistry. Studies on solitons
are very active and the topic has attracted great attention. With the development of soliton theory, several types of soliton solutions
are found, such as simple line solitons, loop solitons, algebraic solitons, breathers, rogue waves, cuspon and peakon solutions, et al. For
(2 + 1)-dimensional integrable equations, for example, Davey–Stewartson (DS) equation and KP equation, there exist localized soliton
solutions, including dromions, lumps and rogue wave solutions. Although mathematical structures and hidden algebraic properties of
soliton equations have attracted more and more attention, investigation of solutions of nonlinear soliton equations is a permanent topic
because these solutions can help us understand nonlinear phenomena.
The structure of soliton equations has been clarified due to various analytic and algebraic methods, such as inverse scattering
method [2], Riemann–Hilbert method [3], Darboux transformation method [4] and Hirota bilinear method [5]. Among those methods,
Hirota bilinear method is purely algebraic and direct. By transforming soliton equations into bilinear forms, we can not only derive
N-soliton solutions and N−periodic wave solutions, but also obtain integrable characteristics such as Bäcklund transformation, Lax
pairs and infinite conservation laws. In general, these soliton solutions own determinant or Pfaffian structures. Sato theory gives deep
understanding of Hirota’s method and present bilinear integrable hierarchy [6]. Recently, breathers and rogue waves have attracted
much attention. There are two kinds of breathers, spacial periodic and time periodic, respectively. In the limit case, breathers could
become rogue waves. Rogue waves originate from oceanography [7] and describe an instantaneous excitation phenomenon. Rogue
wave solutions of integrable systems are rational in both space and time variables. Based on Lax pairs, Darboux transformation method
is useful to construct rational solutions [8–10]. Besides, the KP hierarchy reduction technique based on Hirota bilinear method is also
a powerful approach to generate rational solutions of integrable systems [11–14]. This method has been successfully applied to study
solutions of nonlocal integrable systems [15,16]. Algebraic solitons are some kind of rational solutions and less studied for soliton

equations. They appear in KP equation [17], DS equation [18], the Benjamin–Ono equation [19], a model equation for deep water
waves [20], and also in some nonlocal integrable systems [21,22]. Algebraic solitons also exhibit elastic collision phenomena. It is
interesting to find algebraic soliton solutions for soliton equations. Meanwhile, solutions of integrable systems are mainly on constant
backgrounds, zero or nonzero, and less discussion about solutions on the periodic backgrounds.
The aim of the present paper is devoted to the studies of a (2+1)-dimensional dispersive long wave (DLW) system
uyt + vxx + uuxy + uxuy + 2auxy = 0, (1)
vt + (uv)x + uxxy + 2avx + 2λux = 0, (2)
where λ and a are arbitrary constants. By the variable transformation x → x − 2at, DLW system (1)–(2) reduces to
uyt + vxx + uuxy + uxuy = 0, (3)
vt + (uv)x + uxxy + 2λux = 0, (4)
which was firstly derived by Boiti in 1987 [23] to model the hydrodynamics of a wide channel or open sea with a finite depth. Here
u(x, y, t) is the horizontal velocity and v(x, y, t) is the wave height above the undisturbed water surface. Variables t and (x, y) represent
the time and propagation plane, respectively. Under the dimension reduction y = x, one dimensional DLW system can approximate
the inviscid gravity waves on a water layer with constant depth [24,25].
DLW system has attracted great attention. In [26], Grammian and Wronskian type determinant solutions of DLW system were
presented based on the KP reduction approach. By choosing exponential type determinant elements, N−bright soliton solutions of
DLW were derived [26]. Group theoretical analysis [27], non-Painlevé property [28], Darboux transformation [29,30], Kac–Moody–
Virasoro symmetry algebra [31] and line solitons and dromion solutions [32–34] of DLW system were studied. We will present different
kinds of solutions of DLW system and show that soliton solutions for DLW system given in [34] is a constrained case of our soliton
solutions derived here. Recently, Grinevich and Santini [35] show that the rogue wave solution or the Akhmediev breather solution
on a nonzero background expressed by elliptic function can be formed. Breather and rogue wave solutions for the Hirota equation
and nonlinear Schrödinger equation on the elliptic function background were studied [36,37]. The construction of soliton solutions
on periodic function backgrounds is much more challenge. In this paper, one aim is to construct general solutions, such as solitons,
breathers and algebraic soliton solutions, of DLW system on the periodic function background systematically. Compared with asymptotic
analysis of exponential-type solitons, asymptotic behavior of algebraic soliton solutions is much more difficult to be conducted. We
analyze the asymptotic behaviors of algebraic solitons of DLW system and show that wave peaks evolve along the lines.
The paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we introduce the derivation and reduction of the solutions of DLW system from
the modified KP equations. In Section 3, we analyze the dynamics of solitons on constant and periodic background, respectively. In
Section 4, we investigate the breather solutions. In Section 5, we discuss the dynamic behaviors of the rational solutions which include
algebraic solitons, multiple and high-order rational solutions. Lump solutions are found for some intermediate potential. Section 6 is
devoted to the conclusion and discussion.

6. Conclusion and discussion
The KP hierarchy reduction technique is a powerful method to investigate structures of solutions of integrable systems. Based on
this approach, we investigated a (2+1)-dimensional generalized dispersive wave system. General soliton and (semi-)rational solutions
are given in terms of N ×N determinant. Under different size of the determinant solution, we construct soliton solutions, line breathers,
algebraic solitons, multiple and high-order rational solutions on constant and periodic background, respectively. Dynamics of obtained
solutions with different parameters are analyzed. Roles of constant a in DLW are discussed. As a byproduct, we find lump solutions for
the intermediate potential of DLW system. It is known that the NLS equation possess multi-breather solutions on the elliptic function
background. One question is how to construct breather solutions on the elliptic function background for DLW system. Meanwhile,
nonlocal integrable equations, such as nonlocal NLS and nonlocal mKdV, were proposed from the reduction of well known AKNS system.
These nonlocal systems possess PT symmetries and singular or nonsingular soliton solutions. High dimensional nonlocal integrable
equations, for example, (2+1)-dimensional Davey–Stewartson equation, were also proposed. It would be interesting to investigate
nonlocal DLW system and it remains a topic to be explored in the future.
