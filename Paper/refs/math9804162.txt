                                                                                        Abstract
                                                  A nonlinear transformation of the dispersive long wave equations in (2 + 1) dimensions
                                                  is derived by using the homogeneous balance method. With the aid of the transfor-
                                                  mation given here, exact solutions of the equations are obtained.
