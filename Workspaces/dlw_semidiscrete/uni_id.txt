leading continuum term of X (uniform), computed from the limits:
   B_{a-h}F_{j+1}.G_j   ->  h B_a f_y.g - 2h D_x f.g + O(h^2)
   B_{a+h}F_j.G_{j+1}   ->  h B_a f.g_y + 2h D_x f.g + O(h^2)
   h^2(F_{j+1}G_j-F_jG_{j+1}) -> O(h^3)
   X = h D_y B_a f.g - 4h D_x f.g + O(h^2) = O(h^2)   using (6): D_yB_a f.g = 4D_x f.g
   => X is a HIGHER-ORDER lattice identity: it carries no O(h^0) continuum equation.
   Contrast: the STAGGERED two-point form (star-prime) has leading term
   B_a f.g_y + 2D_x f.g = (6)|lam=-2  at order h^0 after dividing by h.
